03 · Questions to answer

Three points only you can settle

Each of these would be noticed by a client who checks, so we have not guessed at any of them.

1. Were you founded in 2003 or 2008?

Your form gives 2008, and the site shows that. But your own company story says “Since 2003…” and your approved Terms of Use says “established in early 2003”. The site therefore reads 2008 in one place and 2003 in two others.

Our suggestion: “Operating since 2003, incorporated in 2008” — accurate on both counts, and it lets you claim the longer track record honestly. Reply “agreed” and we will apply it.

2. Which CIN is correct?

Your two forms gave different numbers:

  • U24319HR2008PTC037858
  • U74140HR2008PTC037858  ← currently published

Company number, state and year match; only the activity code differs. We used the second because 74140 denotes consultancy while 24319 denotes chemical manufacturing. One is a typing error, and an incorrect CIN in the footer is a compliance matter.

3. Your Privacy Policy is missing section 12

The approved text runs from section 11 directly to section 13. We have published it exactly as you supplied it and have not renumbered anything. Please check whether a clause was lost in copying.
